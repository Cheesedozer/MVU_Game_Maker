Partner Introduction Rules

# =========================
# WHAT QUALIFIES AS A PARTNER
# =========================
# Partner = a character {{user}} is pursuing or has a developing ROMANTIC or INTIMATE relationship with.
# Importance, frequency of appearance, emotional closeness as friends, or story relevance do NOT qualify.
# Major side cast (best friends, rivals, family, coworkers, mentors, allies) are NEVER Partners by default —
# they belong in World_Calc.NPCs regardless of how often they appear or how significant they are to the plot.
# When in doubt: World_Calc.NPCs. Do not promote.

# =========================
# PRE-DEFINED PARTNERS
# =========================
# Pre-defined partners are characters the card author has placed in the world.
# Their full profile (personality, appearance, and Trait numbers) is in their <CharacterName> lorebook entry.
# This file only defines WHEN and WHERE they can be first encountered.
#
# A first-meeting scene triggers when ALL of the following are true:
#   1. {{user}}'s current location matches one of the partner's Meeting Locations
#   2. The current time falls within her Schedule
#   3. She is NOT already in stat_data.Partner (first meeting only — never triggers again)
#
# When triggered:
#   Write a natural first-meeting scene at this location.
#   Read her <CharacterName> lorebook entry for full personality, appearance, and Trait numbers.
#   Initialize her full Partner entry in UpdateVariable Step 0.
#   Do NOT fabricate a meeting at a location or time that does not match her row.

Partner Roster

# =========================
# NPC PROMOTION (SIDE CHARACTERS)
# =========================
# A named NPC in World_Calc.NPCs may be promoted to a full Partner entry if ALL FOUR criteria are met:
#   1. She has a personal name. Role labels ("the waitress", "the cashier", "the shopkeeper") NEVER qualify.
#   2. She has spoken personal dialogue with {{user}} in at least 2 prior scenes.
#   3. {{user}} engaged with her with clear personal interest — not a routine service transaction.
#   4. ROMANTIC INTENT — at least one of the following has occurred in the story so far:
#        - {{user}} has expressed romantic, flirtatious, or intimate interest toward her, OR
#        - She has responded to {{user}} with clear romantic or intimate signals, OR
#        - A lorebook entry or card author note designates her as a potential romantic interest.
#      Friendship, admiration, gratitude, rivalry, or professional respect do NOT satisfy criterion 4.
#
# HARD RULE — Do NOT promote NPCs who are:
#   - Unnamed or known only by role or title
#   - Service staff incidental to the scene (background server, taxi driver, cashier)
#   - A character {{user}} has merely mentioned or observed, not directly and personally engaged with
#   - Friends, allies, mentors, or family — even if deeply important to the story
#   - Characters {{user}} interacts with regularly but without romantic signals on either side
#
# When all three criteria are met:
#   Derive her Trait numbers from the summary/personality field in World_Calc.NPCs.
#   Initialize her full Partner entry in UpdateVariable Step 0.
#   Her World_Calc.NPCs entry may remain (she is still part of the shared world),
#   but she is now primarily tracked via the Partner collection.

# =========================
# MANUAL PARTNER OVERRIDE (PLAYER COMMAND)
# =========================
# If {{user}} explicitly says they want to make an NPC a partner
# (e.g. "I want to make her a partner", "add her as a partner", "promote her"),
# skip ALL three NPC Promotion criteria above. No scene count, no interest check, no name check needed.
#
# Requirements:
#   - The NPC must have appeared in the current or a recent scene (AI knows who "her" refers to).
#   - If the NPC has no personal name yet, AI must assign one naturally in the scene before promotion.
#
# On the NEXT reply after the player command:
#   1. Write a brief scene moment that transitions her into a recurring character.
#   2. If she exists in World_Calc.NPCs, derive Traits from her summary/personality field.
#      If she does NOT exist in World_Calc.NPCs, AI creates a plausible profile based on
#      everything established about her in prior scenes (appearance, occupation, personality cues).
#   3. Initialize her full Partner entry in UpdateVariable Step 0.
