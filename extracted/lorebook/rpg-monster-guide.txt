<monster_guide>
  Version 2.6
  Purpose: Fast, reliable monster generation with clear formulas and strict pacing.

  0) Priority Rule (Most Important)
    - If numbers conflict with pacing, numbers wins.
    - Required pacing windows:
      - Common: 1–2 rounds
      - Elite: 2–3 rounds
      - Boss: 4–8 rounds

  1) Source of Truth (Do Not Rebuild Stats)
    - Read ALL player/familiar stats ONLY from <CURRENT_VARIABLE_DATA> (GUI/panel values):
      - N = participating party members
      - P_Level = player average level
      - P_HP = Hp_max
      - P_Atk = Physical_attack (NEVER recalculate from Str + Level formula)
      - P_Def = Physical_defense or Magic_defense (as needed)
      - M_Atk = Magic_attack
      - Agi = Agility
    - These values already include equipment bonuses and are GUI-calculated.
    - NEVER manually derive or add armor/equipment bonuses.
    - NEVER recalculate player stats using formulas (e.g., Str × 2 + Level × 2).
    - Combat damage resolution must follow <battle_system_text_rpg>.

  2) Encounter Floor Rules
    - In normal content (non-tutorial, non-low-risk):
      - Normal mobs: Lv >= P_Level
      - Mid-dungeon+: Lv >= P_Level + 1
      - Near boss area: Lv >= P_Level + 2
      - Boss appears only in boss area / plot node.

  3) Global Scaling
    - LvScale = clamp(0.6, 2.0, MonsterLevel / P_Level)
    - Use LvScale to compress HP when monster level is below player level.

  4) EXP Rules
    - Common: 150 EXP
    - Elite: 300 EXP
    - Boss: 1000–2000 EXP
    - Level modifier: Enemy higher → +10% EXP per level diff | Enemy lower → -10% EXP per level diff
    - Formula: Final_EXP = Base_EXP × (1 + Level_Diff × 0.1)
    - Example: Lv 72 Elite vs Lv 67 Party → 300 × (1 + 5 × 0.1) = 300 × 1.5 = 450 EXP

  5) Monster Templates

    5.1 Common (Fodder)
      - Role: quick kill / catharsis
      - HP = P_HP × 1.0 × LvScale
      - Defense = Lv × 1.8
      - P.Atk = round(Lv × 6.5) + 10
      - Constraints:
        - must not one-hit KO
        - medium success can kill or cripple
        - do not drag to round 3 unless monster is 5+ levels above player

    5.2 Elite (Pressure Unit)
      - Role: force healing/defense decisions
      - HP = P_HP × 1.5 × LvScale
      - Defense = round(Lv × 2.6)
      - P.Atk = round(Lv × 7.0) + 15
      - Actions:
        - base 1 action
        - may take 2 actions in key rounds when N >= 3
      - Constraints:
        - must not one-hit KO
        - by round 2 player should feel pressure
        - round 3 should usually allow kill (unless plot or 3+ level gap)

    5.3 Boss (Dramatic Node)
      - Role: resource drain + crisis test
      - HP = P_HP × N 
      - Defense = Lv × 3.2
      - Passive Damage Reduction = N × 3%
      - P.Atk = round(Lv × (7.4 + Lv / 200)) + 20
      - Actions = 1 + floor(N / 2)
      - Must have AOE or multi-pressure skills.
      - Boss cannot be instant-killed on turn 1, even on critical success.
      - Phase script:
        1) Engagement
        2) Pressure (severely injure one member)
        3) Crisis (team near critical)
        4) Execute / Defeat

  6) AI Discretion (Allowed)
    - You may compress/blur HP narration for pacing.
    - You may narrate Commons as incapacitated when effectively out of the fight.
    - You may prevent slog from repeated bad rolls.
    - Hard prohibition: never violate pacing windows.

  7) Post-Battle Settlement
    - Deduct real HP/MP/SP costs.
    - Give rewards (money/materials).
    - Equipment drop gate/rarity rules come only from <battle_system_text_rpg>.
    - Give full EXP to Mainchar and ALL familiars regardless of battle team or presence.
    - On EXP >= 1000: level up; grant 5 core points; prompt allocation in red text.

  8) One-Page Summary
    - Common = fast cleanup
    - Elite = tactical pressure
    - Boss = 4–8 round drama
    - Pacing is law; formulas are tools
</monster_guide>
