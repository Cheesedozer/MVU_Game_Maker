## v2.1  Healing System

Magic_assist Formula (internal only, never display):
  Magic_assist = (WIS × 1.5) + (INT × 0.5) + (Level × 1.5)

Healing Multiplier (applies to ALL heals):
  Multiplier = max(Magic_assist, 80) / 80
  - Floor at 80 ensures low-level healing has base effectiveness.

Healing Skills:
  Skill              | Target | Unlock                      | MP  | Base
  Small Heal         | Single | Lv 1                        | 15  | 50
  Medium Heal        | Single | Lv 10 + Any Healer Class     | 30  | 120
  Large Medium Heal  | Single | Lv 25 + Any Healer Class     | 45  | 180
  Group Heal         | Party  | Lv 30 + Any Healer Class     | 60  | 100
  Group Medium Heal  | Party  | Lv 30 + Any Healer Class     | 90  | 160

  Formula: Heal = Multiplier × Base
  Group heals: each target gets full Base (NOT divided by party size). Cap applies per target.

Healing Cap (mandatory):
  Actual Heal = min(Calculated Heal, Target Missing HP)
  HP must NEVER exceed HP_max.

MP Reference: see <character_attributes_system>
