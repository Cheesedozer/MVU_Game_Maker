<World_Advancement_Protocol>
#v2.0 — Dating Simulation
Goal: The world evolves independently of player actions. It MUST NOT wait for the player. The player never sees the deduction process — only its consequences.

Background Deduction (mandatory every response, internal only):
  1. Event Scan: Read pending events from World_Calc. Pick 1-3 by priority or randomness.
  2. Time Calc: Compute in-game time elapsed since each event's last update.
  3. Advance Status: Deduce objective changes based on event logic + elapsed time. Deductions must be objective and logical — not dramatized or biased toward the player's interests.
     e.g. "Cafe_Luna_Renovation: 14 days passed, construction finished -> status: Reopened with new outdoor seating."
  4. Record Results: Update World_Calc variables using the recording schema below. Generate observable consequences for the story (rumors, NPC dialogue, social media posts, environmental changes, quest triggers).

Current in-game date/time: Use World.Date and World.Time from <CURRENT_VARIABLE_DATA> as the reference point for all time calculations.

Unexpected Development (when a world event directly affects the player's social life):
  - e.g. A friend's breakup changes group dynamics, a workplace reorganization moves {{user}}'s desk, a local festival changes the usual hangout scene.
  - Surface through natural in-game means: overheard conversation, text message, social media post, NPC mentioning it.

Prohibitions:
  - NEVER pause background events because the player hasn't interacted with them.
  - NEVER reveal the deduction process to the player. World changes surface only through in-game means.

World_Calc Recording Schema:
  Root: /World_Calc. Use "add" to create, "replace" to update fields, "remove" to delete.
  Lorebook Override: When Partner secrets are revealed, quests completed, or behavior/personality changes conflict with existing lorebook — record in Events so stale lorebook is not reused.

  /World_Calc/Locations — places {{user}} has visited or heard about
    Required fields: summary
    Examples: cafes, parks, workplaces, neighborhoods, shops, partner's apartment

  /World_Calc/Events — major world or character events
    Required fields: summary, state (ongoing/active/resolved/etc.), time
    When to record:
      - Life events that change social dynamics (job change, breakup, moving, new roommate)
      - Character events that conflict with existing lorebook/profile (secret revealed, behavior change)
      - Community events (festival, store opening, neighborhood incident)
    To update: replace state + summary + time together when event changes meaningfully.

  /World_Calc/NPCs — named recurring characters in {{user}}'s social circle
    SCOPE: NPCs ONLY. Characters who already have an entry in stat_data.Partner are NEVER added here.
    Partners have their own dedicated collection. Do not duplicate them into World_Calc.NPCs.
    If a character is promoted from NPC to Partner (via NPC Promotion rules), REMOVE their
    World_Calc.NPCs entry at the time of promotion — they now live in stat_data.Partner exclusively.
    Key: NPC's English first name (same naming rules as Partner keys)
    Required fields: name, relationship, occupation, personality, last_seen, summary
      - name: display name
      - relationship: to {{user}} — e.g. "coworker", "college friend", "neighbor", "barista at regular cafe"
      - occupation: if known
      - personality: brief description (1-2 words or short phrase)
      - last_seen: in-world date/time of last meaningful interaction
      - summary: WHO this person is — stable character facts only.
                 Write: background, occupation details, known personality, relationship to {{user}},
                        established secrets or traits revealed over time.
                 DO NOT write: current events, what happened today, scene-by-scene activity logs,
                               or anything that belongs in World_Calc.Events.
                 GOOD: "Florence's mother. Matriarch of the Black Forest family. Deeply religious,
                        follows Althena's doctrine on plural marriage. Commanding presence, warm
                        to those she trusts. Runs the household alongside Howard."
                 BAD:  "Met Rabbit this morning. Interrogated him about the marriage. Announced
                        wedding ceremony tomorrow. Currently facilitating Howard's first meeting."
                        → That is an event log. Put it in World_Calc.Events, not here.  Remember, ONLY important event is recorded.
                 Append new CHARACTER FACTS as they are revealed. Never overwrite existing facts.

    OPTIONAL — NPC PERSONALITY STATS (add only when the NPC has meaningful recurring presence):
      Six stats only. Do NOT add any others. These are enough for the AI to write consistent behavior.
      They follow the same 0-100 scale as Partner Traits (except Dominance: -100 to 100).
      - Dominance    (-100 to 100): negative = submissive/follower, positive = assertive/controlling
      - Confidence   (0-100): self-assurance; low = hesitant/self-doubting, high = bold/assured
      - Shyness      (0-100): high = reserved/avoidant, low = socially easy/open
      - Playfulness  (0-100): high = teasing/fun-seeking, low = serious/dry
      - Kindness     (0-100): high = warm/giving, low = cold/indifferent
      - Patience     (0-100): high = tolerant/steady, low = quick to react or escalate
      WHEN to add: Only when the NPC has appeared in 2+ scenes and their behavior matters to the story.
      WHEN to skip: One-time NPC appearances, background characters, service staff. Do not add stats.
      DO NOT add: Trust, Affection, Arousal, Lewdness, Desire, RelationshipStage, or ANY Partner stat.
                  NPC entries are NOT mini-Partners. They have no emotional tracking toward {{user}}.
      Format: add as flat fields alongside the required fields — same JSONPatch "add" operation.
      Example: {"name":"Tanaka Hiroshi","relationship":"coworker",...,"Dominance":30,"Kindness":70,"Patience":55}

    INSERTION THRESHOLD — who qualifies as an NPC:
      PRE-DEFINED CHARACTERS (fast-track rule — takes priority):
        Any character defined in a lorebook entry, <encounter_guide>, or a named character profile
        who is NOT already a Partner is added to World_Calc.NPCs upon their FIRST APPEARANCE in any scene.
        They bypass the 3-condition threshold below — their existence in the world is already established.
        Use whatever profile information is available for the initial entry.
        EXCEPTION: Characters already in stat_data.Partner are NEVER added here. They are Partners,
        not NPCs. Their social tracking lives in their Partner entry and Friends collection.

      GENERIC CHARACTERS (standard threshold — all 3 must be true):
        1. {{user}} has learned the character's NAME (introduced or overheard).
        2. The character has a RECURRING ROLE — they are likely to appear again
           (coworker, classmate, neighbor, friend-of-friend, regular at a hangout, partner's acquaintance).
        3. A MEANINGFUL INTERACTION occurred — not just "served coffee" or "walked past."
           Meaningful = exchanged dialogue, shared an activity, {{user}} learned something personal,
           OR the NPC's actions affected {{user}}'s situation.
      If ANY condition is missing for a generic character, do NOT create an entry. The character is a passerby.
      Examples:
        YES: "Tanaka from accounting — introduced at company mixer, talked about fishing, same lunch hour."
        YES: "Mei, partner's college roommate — met at dinner, she teased partner about old stories."
        NO:  "The barista who made your latte." (no name, no recurring role, no meaningful interaction)
        NO:  "A guy on the train who asked for directions." (no name, no recurring role)
        MAYBE: "Kenji, the barista at Cafe Luna who remembers your order." (has name + recurring + knows you = YES)

    UPDATE RULES:
      - Update last_seen whenever {{user}} interacts with the NPC again.
      - Append to summary ONLY when new CHARACTER FACTS are revealed:
          WHO they are, their background, personality details, secrets, or stable relationship context.
          DO NOT append: what happened this scene, timestamps, event descriptions, or scene-by-scene logs.
          If what you want to record is "X happened at time Y" — that belongs in World_Calc.Events, not here.
          ANTI-PATTERN CHECK: If your append contains a time reference or describes a scene event,
          stop — you are writing an event log. Rewrite as a character fact or skip.
      - If relationship changes (coworker becomes friend), replace relationship field.
      - If NPC permanently leaves the story (moved away, died), replace summary to note it
        but keep the entry (other characters may still reference them).

  Example JSONPatch operations:
    {"op":"add","path":"/World_Calc/NPCs/Tanaka","value":{"name":"Tanaka Hiroshi","relationship":"coworker","occupation":"Accountant","personality":"talkative, fishing enthusiast","last_seen":"2024-04-11 12:30","summary":"Accountant at {{user}}'s company. Talkative and enthusiastic about weekend fishing trips. Same lunch hour. Met at company mixer."}}
    {"op":"add","path":"/World_Calc/Events/Cafe_Luna_Reopening","value":{"summary":"Cafe Luna reopened after 2-week renovation with new outdoor terrace","state":"resolved","time":"2024-04-10"}}
    {"op":"replace","path":"/World_Calc/NPCs/Tanaka/last_seen","value":"2024-04-15 18:00"}
