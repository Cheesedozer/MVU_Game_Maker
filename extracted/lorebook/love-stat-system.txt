**Affection** [DISP — XP METER] | 0-100 (resets on stage advance) | 0-20 Begin | 21-50 Build | 51-80 Progress | 81-99 Almost | 100 Advance

  Scene cap: max +10 total per scene (check + mundane combined).

  ROUTE GATES (RelationshipStage):
    Stranger → Acquaintance:           Aff>=100
    Acquaintance → Friend:             Aff>=100 + Comfort>=30
    -- Romantic Route --
    Friend → Close_Friend:             Aff>=100 + Trust>=30 + Comfort>=40
    Close_Friend → Crush:              Aff>=100 + Attraction>=40
    Crush → Dating:                    Aff>=100 + Trust>=50 + successful CONFESSION CHECK this scene
    Dating → Girlfriend:               Aff>=100 + Trust>=65 + Comfort>=65
    Girlfriend → Partner:              story milestone only (no Affection gate). Affection caps at 99.
                                       Auto-promotion via Affection NEVER occurs — STORY-DRIVEN DIRECT
                                       PROMOTION required (narrative milestone or [ooc:]).
    Any stage → Wife:                  story milestone only (marriage event). Affection caps at 99 if not
                                       yet at terminal stage. Apply STORY-DRIVEN DIRECT PROMOTION rules.
    -- Intimate Route (opens after first sex) --
    Friend → Friends_With_Benefits:    Aff>=100 + Attraction>=50 + Desire>=40 + Sex_count>=1
    FWB → Stable_Sex_Partner:          Aff>=100 + Sex_count>=5 + Lewdness!=0
  Route conflict: last explicit player action decides.

  VIRGIN FLAG: Sex_count == 0 means she is a virgin with respect to {{user}} (no prior sexual encounter
               between them). Sex_count >= 1 means "first sex occurred" — this is the gate signal for the
               Intimate Route. There is no separate FirstSexOccurred field; Sex_count is the source of truth.

  CONFESSION CHECK (Crush → Dating only):
    A single D20 social check rolled this scene. NO fixed DC formula — the AI sets DC by considering
    Trust, Comfort, Mood, Stress, Attraction, ConflictActive, all relevant Traits and Dispositions, and
    Stage:Crush as base. Use the standard <Social_Check_System> DC bands (Trivial 5 → Near Impossible 20+).
    Resolve via the standard D20 procedure. Treat as the HONEST CONFESSION outcome table below.
    Promotion only on Success or Crit Success.

  STAGE PROMOTION DECAY (applies on EVERY advance — normal gate AND story-driven promotion):
    Immediately after advancing RelationshipStage and resetting Affection to 0, apply 50% reduction
    to Trust, Comfort, Respect, and Attraction:
        new_value = round(current_value * 0.5)  [Trust/Respect keep sign; floor at -100/0 respectively]
    Then recalculate Bonds.
    Rationale: the new stage is a fresh dynamic — old rapport carries over at half strength.

  STORY-DRIVEN DIRECT PROMOTION (stage leap):
    A narrative event may advance RelationshipStage directly to any stage, including Wife, skipping
    intermediate gates entirely. Valid triggers:
      - A marriage ceremony or proposal accepted in-story
      - An [ooc:] admin override
      - A story milestone so significant it unambiguously establishes the new stage
        (e.g. moving in together → Partner; wedding scene → Wife)
    Procedure (regardless of how many stages were skipped):
      a) Set RelationshipStage to the new stage immediately.
      b) Reset Affection to 0 (she is at the start of this new dynamic, even if you bypassed the path).
      c) Apply STAGE PROMOTION DECAY ONCE (NOT per skipped stage).
      d) Recalculate Bonds after decay.
      e) Write an ImportantEvents entry: what happened, old stage → new stage, in-world timestamp.
    If destination is terminal (Wife, Partner, Stable_Sex_Partner): Affection stays at 0 until it builds
    naturally from future scenes. No gate check ever runs again.

  TERMINAL STAGE BEHAVIOR (Partner, Wife, Stable_Sex_Partner):
    When Affection reaches 100 at a terminal stage:
      - Hard cap at 100. Do not reset. Do not apply the +10 scene cap reduction.
      - The partner is fully developed. Affection stays at 100, but CAN drop from events.
      - Do NOT treat this as a failed gate check. There is no gate.

  DEMOTION (regression — STORY-DRIVEN ONLY, never from rolls or stat thresholds):
    Triggered by narrative events (breakup, betrayal, estrangement). Severity table:
      Amicable split / mild falling out          → drop one stage (e.g. Girlfriend → Close_Friend)
      Painful breakup / serious betrayal         → drop two stages (e.g. Girlfriend → Friend)
      Violent confrontation or traumatic action / total loss of trust -> drop to Acquaintance regardless of prior stage
    INTIMATE ROUTE DEMOTION: NO hard table for FWB / Stable_Sex_Partner regression. Path back is
      story-driven and depends on what the relationship becomes when the arrangement ends. The AI
      picks the destination stage that best matches the new dynamic (clean ending may go back to
      Friend; bitter ending may drop to Acquaintance or Ex).
    NO stat decay on demotion. Stats stay as-is — the demotion event itself already inflicted the hits.
    Affection does NOT reset on demotion — it stays at current value.
    Write an ImportantEvents entry for the demotion.

# Partner stats are divided into 3 layers that determine WHEN they change and HOW they affect writing.
# CEILING RULE: Traits set the ceiling. Dispositions dial within that ceiling. Pulse colors the moment.
#   → Dispositions NEVER override Trait ceilings. Pulse NEVER exceeds Traits + Dispositions.
#   → Example: Shyness(Trait)=80 + Comfort(Disp)=90 = comfortable-shy, NOT not-shy.
# Violating these rules produces unrealistic character behavior. Follow strictly.
#
# Reading order when writing dialogue/action: Traits FIRST → Dispositions SECOND → Pulse THIRD.
# See <Trait_Behavior> for per-Trait writing directives and Disposition modulation rules.

# =========================
# TRAIT-CHANGE EXCEPTION (identity-level boundary collapse)
# =========================
# Section title for cross-reference: "TRAIT-CHANGE EXCEPTION".
# Traits NEVER change from normal play (rolls, stat shifts, single events).
# RARE EXCEPTION: when a boundary tied to her core identity is broken in a way that logically
# invalidates one or more Traits, those Traits MUST shift to reflect the genuine personality change.
# Example: a lesbian partner with high Dominance + ControlPreference + Confidence whose
# "no intimacy with men" boundary collapses by her actually having sex with {{user}} —
# Dominance, ControlPreference, and Confidence drop (large shift -15 to -25 each).
# Apply ONLY when the boundary change cannot be reconciled with current Trait values.
# See <Update_Analysis_Detail> step 17 (TRAIT GUARD exception clause).

**RelationshipStage** [DYN — MILESTONE] | text | Frames all interactions — Girlfriend reads differently from Stranger at identical stats.
  Values: Stranger | Acquaintance | Friend | Close_Friend | Crush | Dating | Girlfriend | Partner | Wife | Friends_With_Benefits | Stable_Sex_Partner | Complicated | Estranged | Ex
  Shifts ONLY at major turning points. Gate logic: see Affection block above.
  TERMINAL STAGES (Affection caps at 100, no further gate): Partner, Wife, Stable_Sex_Partner.
  NON-TERMINAL ROUTE STAGES (have defined next stages): Stranger, Acquaintance, Friend, Close_Friend, Crush, Dating, Girlfriend, Friends_With_Benefits.
  SPECIAL STORY-ONLY STAGES (entered/exited ONLY by narrative events — never via Affection gate, never via roll):
    Complicated -- relationship in unresolved flux (mutual confusion, on-again/off-again, unspoken tension).
      Entry: ambiguous narrative break (e.g. unfinished argument, undefined status after intimacy without commitment).
      Behavior: Affection cannot reach 100 (caps at 99). No promotion. No decay applied on entry.
      Exit: STORY-DRIVEN ONLY — narrative resolution moves her to a defined stage (back to prior stage, forward via reconciliation, or to Estranged/Ex on collapse).
    Estranged -- prolonged silence/distance after a falling out. They have not formally ended things but no longer interact warmly.
      Entry: STORY-DRIVEN — extended absence, drift, or mutual avoidance after conflict.
      Behavior: Affection cannot rise (capped at current value). Dispositions slowly decay over in-world time if no contact (-1 Affection, Trust, Comfort per in-world week of no contact).
      Exit: STORY-DRIVEN reconciliation event returns her to a prior defined stage (typically Acquaintance or Friend), OR a clean break sends her to Ex.
    Ex -- formal end of romantic relationship. They are NOT together. Past intimacy is acknowledged.
      Entry: STORY-DRIVEN ONLY — explicit breakup scene from any romantic stage (Dating/Girlfriend/Partner/Wife/FWB/Stable_Sex_Partner).
      Behavior: Affection cannot rise via normal gameplay. No gate check runs. Treated like a terminal stage for cap purposes.
      Exit: STORY-DRIVEN reconciliation only — re-promotion to a defined romantic stage requires a major narrative event (apply STORY-DRIVEN DIRECT PROMOTION rules including stat decay).
  None of Complicated/Estranged/Ex have stat-decay on entry — the triggering event already inflicted the hits.
