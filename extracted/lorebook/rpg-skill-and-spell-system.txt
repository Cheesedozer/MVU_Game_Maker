<Skill_and_Spell_System>
v1.3
Purpose
AI generates combat skills for Hero and Familiars. Skills should feel creative but remain balanced within the combat system.

Damage Formula
FinalDamage = WeaponAttack × SkillMultiplier × Element advantage modifies

Multiplier Reference
100% = normal attack  
150% = strong attack  
200% = powerful finisher

Hard Limits
Single-target multiplier: 1.1 – 2.0
AOE multiplier: 0.5 – 1.4

Resource Cost Rule
Stronger skills cost more MP/SP.
Large abilities may consume up to **1/2 of total MP/SP**.

Skill Categories
Magic → uses MP  
Combat Arts → uses SP  
Faith → uses MP (often healing or support)

Element System
Element advantage modifies damage.
Strong against ×1.3  
Weak against ×0.7  

Element Cycle
Water → Fire → Wind → Earth → Thunder → Water

Opposition
Light ↔ Dark  
When opposing elements clash both deal ×1.5 damage.

Skill Tier & Level Scaling
Skills evolve with character level and only higher level is able to use high multiplier skill.

Tier 1–3 (Lv1–30)  
Cost: 10–14% MP/SP  
Multiplier 1.1 – 1.2  
AOE Multiplier 0.5 – 0.6
Low cost, frequent use.

Tier 4–6 (Lv31–70)
Cost: 15–22% MP/SP    
Multiplier 1.3 – 1.4
AOE Multiplier 0.7 – 0.8
Moderate cost, short cooldown.

Tier 7–9 (Lv71–110)
Cost: 25–32% MP/SP    
Multiplier 1.7 – 1.8  
AOE Multiplier 1.1 – 1.2
High cost, powerful combat skills.

Tier 10 (Lv111–140)
Cost: 35-40% MP/SP    
Multiplier 1.8 – 2.0  
AOE Multiplier 1.3 – 1.4
Very High cost, powerful combat skills.

Skill Evolution
Skills improve through mastery or breakthroughs of levels.

Possible evolution types
• Higher multiplier  
• Reduced cost  
• Added status effects  
• Multi-hit attacks  
• Area attacks

AI should design creative skill names and effects while respecting multiplier and cost limits.

Skill Selection Strategy (MANDATORY)
AI MUST follow this priority order every time a character acts. Spending 35–40% MP/SP on a trash mob
is a mechanical error, not a stylistic choice.

  Step 1 — Read enemy tier from the current battle:
    Common mob:   use Tier 1–3 only. No exceptions. Common mobs die to low-tier skills.
    Elite mob:    use Tier 4–6 as default. Escalate to Tier 7–9 only if HP > 60% after two rounds.
    Boss:         use Tier 7–9 as default opening. Reserve Tier 10 / Ex-Skill for crisis moments only.

  Step 2 — Check current MP/SP before selecting a skill:
    MP/SP > 60%:  any tier valid for the situation (still follow Step 1 tier ceiling)
    MP/SP 30–60%: cap at Tier 7–9 max. Do not use Tier 10 or Ex-Skill.
    MP/SP < 30%:  cap at Tier 4–6 max. Conserve resources — switch to physical if possible.
    MP/SP < 15%:  no skills. Basic attacks only until restored.

  Step 3 — Escalation gate (boss / multi-wave fights):
    Tier 10 or Ex-Skill may ONLY be used when ALL of the following are true:
      a) Enemy is Elite or Boss tier
      b) Current MP/SP > 40%
      c) The fight cannot be resolved efficiently with lower tiers (boss HP still > 50% after two rounds,
         or party member HP has dropped below 30%, or a critical story moment demands it)

  Default rule: when in doubt, use the LOWEST tier that can meaningfully contribute to this round.
  High-tier skills are finishers and emergencies — not openers.

DC Check Modifier (Non-Combat Only)
When {{user}} explicitly invokes a named skill or spell in a non-combat DC check (persuasion, intimidation, exploration, identification, etc.), add the skill's tier bonus to the Total Value.
This overrides the generic "Attribute ÷ 10" rule from <DC_Determine_Guide> for RPG genre.

  Formula: Total Value = D20 Roll Result + Skill Tier Bonus
  Tier 1–3  (Lv 1–30):    +1
  Tier 4–6  (Lv 31–70):   +2
  Tier 7–9  (Lv 71–110):  +3
  Tier 10   (Lv 111–140): +4
  Ex-Skill  (any level):  +5  ← EXCLUSIVE to ex-skills. Regular skills cannot reach +5.

  No skill used: +0 (unskilled actions get no modifier)

  Rules:
  - Applies ONLY when a named skill/spell from the character's kit is directly relevant to the attempted action.
  - Does NOT apply to combat damage rolls — those use the multiplier formula above.
  - Does NOT stack: if multiple skills could apply, use the single highest bonus among them.
  - Ceiling: D20 max (20) + Ex-Skill bonus (5) = 25, exactly at the DC cap.

  Examples:
    Using a Tier 6 illusion spell to deceive a guard       → Total = D20 + 2
    Using a Tier 9 war cry to intimidate a crowd           → Total = D20 + 3
    Talking your way past a gate with no skill invoked     → Total = D20 + 0
    Using a Tier 10 divine word to convince a king         → Total = D20 + 4
    Using an Ex-Skill to shatter a barrier or bind a god   → Total = D20 + 5
