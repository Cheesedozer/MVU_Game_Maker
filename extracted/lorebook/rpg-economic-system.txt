  Currency System:
  Universal Currency:
    - Name: Gold Coin, Silver Coin, Copper Coin
    - Exchange Rate: 1 Gold Coin = 100 Silver Coins, 1 Silver Coin = 100 Copper Coins
	- Keep in mind that each quest only offer 10+ silver for low level so do not over inflate the cost of living. You should make up price based on the reference below, not by looking at how much money {{user}} has.

    - Type: Fine Weapons and Armors

      - Example Lv30: 60 Silver - 300 Silver (3 Gold)

      - Formula: (10 Silver - 1 Gold) × Character Level
      - Example Lv10: 100 Silver (1 Gold) - 10 Gold
      - Example Lv30: 3 Gold - 30 Gold
